A cafe, also known as a coffeehouse or coffee shop, is a type of establishment primarily serving coffee and tea, along with light refreshments like pastries and snacks. Cafes offer a casual social environment for activities like reading, studying, or chatting with others. The term "cafe" originates from the French word for "coffee".
